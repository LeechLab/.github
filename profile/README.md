### _By gamers, for gamers._

LeechLab creates games for players to enjoy. We craft masterful games with focus, effort, and vision, all for the love of the game.

---

## About Us

We're a passion-driven game studio. We don't chase money; we chase great games. We want to push innovation forward, and we make games because we love making them and want to share that passion with the world.

## Our Goals

- **Innovate:** Push ideas, mechanics, and technology forward.
- **Create with passion:** Every project is built out of love for the game, not for profit.
- **Share the experience:** Bring our games to as many players as possible, on as many platforms as possible.
- **Build with craft:** Focus, effort, and vision in everything we ship.

## What We Make

We don't limit ourselves to a single genre. If it sparks our interest, we'll build it.

| Genre              | Examples of what we explore               |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| FPS                | Fast, competitive first-person action     |
| Top-down .io games | Quick-join, browser-friendly multiplayer  |
| Sports games       | Arcade and competitive sports experiences |
| Everything else    | If we like it, we make it                 |

## Platforms

We aim to be everywhere players are:
